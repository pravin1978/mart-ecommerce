Refund Policy

Our refund policy for digital products purchased on Digimart.

Last updated: October 15, 2025

Demo policy: this is a demonstration marketplace. No real purchases, payments, orders or deliveries take place, so no refunds apply. The guarantee windows below describe the intended live policy and will take effect at launch.

7-Day Guarantee — eBooks & Audio

eBooks and audio products are covered by a 7-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied, contact us within 7 days of purchase for a full refund.

14-Day Guarantee — Software & Templates

Software and template products are covered by a 14-day money-back guarantee. Products must be returned in their original condition and you must not have downloaded or installed the software.

30-Day Guarantee — Courses

Online courses are covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee. You must not have completed more than 30% of the course content to be eligible for a refund.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund once live purchases launch, email the support address published at that time with your order number and reason. Refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method. Demo Contact Details — Replace Before Launch: support@digimart.com is a placeholder.

Non-Refundable Items

Products that have been downloaded, installed, or accessed (for courses) beyond 30% completion are not eligible for refunds. Custom or personalized digital products are also non-refundable.
