Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how we protect your data at Digimart.

Last updated: October 15, 2025

1. Introduction

Digimart ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we will collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when the live marketplace launches. This demo site places no real orders and processes no real payments.

2. Information We Collect

When the live marketplace launches, we will collect personally identifiable information including your name, email address, account credentials, and purchase history. Payment information will be processed securely through encrypted third-party gateways — no payment gateway is connected to this demo.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to process and deliver your digital product purchases, send download links and license keys, provide customer support, improve our marketplace, and send transactional emails and optional promotions.

4. Data Storage and Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. Your data is stored securely using Firebase, which provides enterprise-grade security including encryption at rest and in transit. All data transmissions use SSL/TLS encryption.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only with payment processors (to securely process transactions), email service providers (to deliver product emails), when required by law, or in the event of a business transfer.

6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to remember your preferences, analyze website traffic, and provide personalized product recommendations. You can disable cookies through your browser settings.
